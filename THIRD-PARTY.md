# Third-party software and art in Kestrel Isle

Kestrel Isle bundles the libraries below, unmodified apart from one shader: `Common/MatDefs/Light/SPLighting.frag` from jMonkeyEngine, with a duplicate set of fog uniform declarations removed.

## KayKit model packs by Kay Lousberg (CC0)

The town buildings come from the KayKit Medieval Hexagon pack by Kay Lousberg (www.kaylousberg.com), released under Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0): free to use in any project, no attribution required. Thank you, Kay.
